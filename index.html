The origin

Born from the work. Built over 20 years.

Lively isn't like any other agency because its founder started inside the real marketing challenges of companies that couldn't afford guesswork and couldn't afford to keep doing what everyone else was doing.

Most agencies were built to serve the Fortune 1,000. Lively was built for the businesses with the most to gain. That gap is why we exist. And the story of how we got here is a little more human than most.

2002 Virgin asked me to set up my own business. Not an offer you get every day. One of the first brand experience agencies of its kind and where CASiTM was actually born, I just didn't know it yet.
2002–2017 Built ITCH, a disruptor agency doing work most agencies wouldn't know what to do with.
2017 Pivoted to Lively: expanding into the US with Spotify, Ericsson, Coinbase and Degreed.
2023 Took a deliberate step back, every agency was offering the same thing, and I could see the tidal wave of AI coming. Kept working with key clients while testing whether my own marketing could prove what I was building.
2024 Launched Audience101 and my own event series; Staying Human in a Digital World, across SXSW and virtually.
2025 Listened. To CEOs, CMOs and specialists all wrestling with the same challenges.

FAQs

Common questions.

Straight answers to what CEOs and founders ask before booking a conversation.

What is Lively?

Lively helps CEOs and founders of growing businesses get clarity on what their marketing is actually doing, what is wasting money, and what needs to happen next.

We work inside the business to connect strategy, audience, marketing activity, and revenue goals into one operating rhythm.

What is CASiTM?

CASiTM is Lively's marketing operating system.

It gives CEOs and marketing leaders a clearer way to see what is working, what is not, and where to focus. It connects strategy, audience, content, campaigns, community, and revenue into a cadence your business can actually run.

Who is Lively for?

Lively is built for CEOs, founders, and leadership teams of businesses doing roughly $5M–$30M in revenue who are spending on marketing but do not have enough clarity on what it is producing.

It is for businesses with momentum, ambition, and complexity, but not enough connection between marketing activity and business results.

What problem does Lively solve?

Most growing businesses are doing a lot of marketing, but the work often becomes scattered.

Channels multiply. Agencies come and go. Content gets produced. Budgets get spent. But the CEO still cannot clearly see what is driving growth.

Lively solves that by creating clarity, direction, and cadence.

How is this different from hiring a marketing agency?

An agency usually executes campaigns or manages specific channels.

Lively works at the operating level. We help leadership understand what marketing is doing, where the waste is, what should stop, what should scale, and how the work connects to revenue.

Less "more marketing stuff." More "better marketing decisions."

How is this different from traditional consulting?

Traditional consulting often ends with a deck.

Lively works inside the business on a regular rhythm. The goal is not just to explain the problem. The goal is to help leadership make decisions, align the team, and keep the system moving.

What do you mean by clarity, direction, cadence?

Clarity means knowing what your marketing is actually doing and where money, time, or energy is being wasted.

Direction means knowing what to focus on next and why it matters.

Cadence means having a rhythm for reviewing strategy, adjusting tactics, and keeping everyone aligned.

Do you replace our marketing team?

No.

Lively is not designed to replace your team. It helps your team, agencies, or partners work from a clearer strategy and shared definition of success.

The goal is to make the people you already have more focused and more effective.

What happens in the first conversation?

The first conversation is a clear, honest read on where your marketing is right now.

We look at what you are doing, what may be costing you, where the gaps are, and what the next best move could be.

No pitch theatre. No "let me take this back to the team" fog machine. Just a useful conversation.
